## Return my XP farm

Drops XP and gold from zombified piglins even when they weren't killed by a player, as long as they were angry at one.  
Fixes automatic gold farms where piglins are killed by fall damage or other non-player means.

Available for Fabric [1.21.5+] and NeoForge [26.1+].
